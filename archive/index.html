NON-MARXISTS · 20 FILES

Filed outside the Index of Marxists: the philosophers and economists Marx worked from, the utopians and anarchists he argued with, the revisionists, and the leaders who governed in Lenin's name after him. Read them to know what Marxism answered.

FILE 01THE PHILOSOPHERS

Immanuel Kant

1724 — 1804 · KÖNIGSBERG

Königsberg philosopher whose critical philosophy set the problems that Hegel answered. A century later, Bernstein and the neo-Kantians called for a return to Kant.

  • The Critique of Pure Reason (1781)TXT
  • An Answer to the Question: What Is Enlightenment? (1784)
  • Fundamental Principles of the Metaphysic of Morals (1785)TXT

Young Hegelian who turned Hegel's philosophy into a materialism of man and nature. Marx's Theses on Feuerbach and Engels's Ludwig Feuerbach settle accounts with him.

  • Towards a Critique of Hegel's Philosophy (1839)

FILE 09THE ANARCHISTS

Pierre-Joseph Proudhon

  • What Is Property? (1840)

FILE 14THE REVISIONISTS

1850 — 1932 · BERLIN

Engels's literary executor, who argued from 1896 that socialism would come by gradual reform. His party rejected the revision, and Luxemburg answered it in Reform or Revolution.
