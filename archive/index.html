Index of Non-Marxists 30 PEOPLE, BY GROUP

The Index of Marxists is this archive's judgement of who kept to Marx's programme. Everyone else here, the thinkers Marxism came out of, its rivals, and the parties and states that governed in its name, is filed in this index, and their works are listed just as fully. The Marxists Internet Archive draws a similar line: it keeps Stalin and Mao in its Reference Archive, apart from its Marxist writers.

THE PHILOSOPHERS

Spinoza, Kant and the Young Hegelians: the philosophy Marx came out of and broke with, outside Hegel himself.

Baruch Spinoza1632–1677Dutch philosopher, expelled from Amsterdam's Jewish community in 1656, whose Ethics identifies God with nature. Hegel held that every philosopher must begin from Spinoza.1 FULL TEXT · 1 WORK Immanuel Kant1724–1804Königsberg philosopher whose critical philosophy set the problems that Hegel answered. A century later, Bernstein and the neo-Kantians called for a return to Kant.2 FULL TEXTS · 3 WORKS Ludwig Feuerbach1804–1872Young Hegelian who turned Hegel's philosophy into a materialism of man and nature. Marx's Theses on Feuerbach and Engels's Ludwig Feuerbach settle accounts with him.1 FULL TEXT · 3 WORKS Bruno Bauer1809–1882Young Hegelian theologian and critic of the Gospels, Marx's teacher and friend in Berlin, whom Marx answered in On the Jewish Question and, with Engels, in The Holy Family.4 WORKS Moses Hess1812–1875Philosopher and socialist who won Engels over to communism in 1842 and worked with Marx in the 1840s; later the author of Rome and Jerusalem (1862), an early work of Zionism.2 WORKS
