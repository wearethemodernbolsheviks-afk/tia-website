FILE 09THE ANARCHISTS

Pierre-Joseph Proudhon

1809 — 1865 · BESANÇON → PASSY

French printer who declared that property is theft and was the first to call himself an anarchist. Marx answered his System of Economic Contradictions with The Poverty of Philosophy.

  • What Is Property? (1840)
  • System of Economical Contradictions, or The Philosophy of Poverty (1846)
  • General Idea of the Revolution in the Nineteenth Century (1851)

FILE 14THE REVISIONISTS

1850 — 1932 · BERLIN

Engels's literary executor, who argued from 1896 that socialism would come by gradual reform. His party rejected the revision, and Luxemburg answered it in Reform or Revolution.

  • Socialism in the German Reichstag and on the German Throne (1890)
