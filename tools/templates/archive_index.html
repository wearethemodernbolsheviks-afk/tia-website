SUBJECT 01PRECURSOR
HEX 3,461

G. W. F. HEGEL

1770 — 1831 · STUTTGART → BERLIN

German idealist philosopher. Marx took his dialectic and set it on a materialist footing.

  • Phenomenology of Spirit (1807)
  • Science of Logic (1812–16)
  • Philosophy of Right (1820)

SUBJECT 02FOUNDER
HEX 3,740

KARL MARX

1818 — 1883 · TRIER → LONDON

Founded scientific socialism with Engels. Spent his last decades on Capital.

  • The Communist Manifesto (1848)
  • The Eighteenth Brumaire (1852)
  • Capital, Volume I (1867)

SUBJECT 03FOUNDER
HEX 3,467

FRIEDRICH ENGELS

1820 — 1895 · BARMEN → LONDON

Marx's partner for forty years. Edited volumes II and III of Capital after Marx died.

  • The Condition of the Working Class in England (1845)
  • Anti-Dühring (1878)
  • The Origin of the Family (1884)

SUBJECT 04SECOND INTERNATIONAL
HEX 3,355

KARL KAUTSKY

1854 — 1938 · PRAGUE → AMSTERDAM

Leading theorist of the German SPD and the Second International.

  • The Class Struggle (1892)
  • The Agrarian Question (1899)
  • The Road to Power (1909)

SUBJECT 05BOLSHEVIK
HEX 3,126

V. I. LENIN

1870 — 1924 · SIMBIRSK → GORKI

Led the Bolshevik party and the October Revolution of 1917.

  • What Is to Be Done? (1902)
  • Imperialism, the Highest Stage of Capitalism (1917)
  • The State and Revolution (1917)

SUBJECT 06BOLSHEVIK
HEX 2,883

LEON TROTSKY

1879 — 1940 · YANOVKA → COYOACÁN

Chaired the Petrograd Soviet in 1905 and 1917 and organised the Red Army.

  • Results and Prospects (1906)
  • History of the Russian Revolution (1930–32)
  • The Revolution Betrayed (1936)

SUBJECT 07SPARTACIST
HEX 3,003

ROSA LUXEMBURG

1871 — 1919 · ZAMOŚĆ → BERLIN

Co-founded the Spartacus League. Murdered in Berlin in January 1919.

  • Reform or Revolution (1899)
  • The Mass Strike (1906)
  • The Accumulation of Capital (1913)

SUBJECT 08BOLSHEVIK
HEX 4,654

N. I. BUKHARIN

1888 — 1938 · MOSCOW → MOSCOW

Bolshevik theorist and editor of Pravda. Shot in 1938 after the third Moscow trial. In USSR 2089, he lives.

  • Imperialism and World Economy (1915)
  • The ABC of Communism (1919)
  • Economics of the Transformation Period (1920)

First leader of the Communist Party of Italy in 1921, and the leading figure of the Italian Communist Left.

  • Lyon Theses (1926)
  • Dialogue with Stalin (1952)
